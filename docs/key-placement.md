# Key placement rules (from Tiago, 2026-11-20)

The wizard suggestions come from the guide layouts. Tiago tested the wizard and found the suggestions
not useful. These are his rules for where each kind of spell goes. They come from his own Warlock setup,
which he plays every day.

## The rules

- **One-button rotation on Mouse 4.** A `/castsequence` of the core rotation on one press. Players
  without side mouse buttons use key 1.
- **Crowd control on E.** The key for emergencies. E is one of the easiest keys to press for almost
  everyone.
- **Q, R and F are the next easiest keys.** They hold the main DoTs and the spells that are pressed
  the most, in almost every fight.
- **R is the "ultimate" key** (like in MOBA games): spells that do a lot of damage, and procs.
- **Wand Shoot on Mouse 5 for casters** (Mage, Priest, Warlock). It is mandatory at low level, and
  the fallback when mana runs out.
- **V holds the Healthstone and potions.** It is not that easy to reach, but it is in a good spot
  (right under F).
